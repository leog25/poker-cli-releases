# Terms of use

poker is a free, play-money No-Limit Hold'em game. By creating an account you
agree to these terms.

## Play money only

Chips have no value. They can't be bought, sold, traded or cashed out, and no
real money is ever involved. Balances, refills and the leaderboard may be reset
at any time.

## Who can play

You must be 18 or older. One account per person.

## Fair play

Don't cheat. That includes:

- sharing cards or strategy with other players at your table (collusion),
- playing more than one account, or letting software play for you,
- exploiting bugs instead of reporting them,
- harassing other players, or using offensive usernames, table names or chat.

Admins may kick you from a table, close tables, remove chips won unfairly, or
ban or delete accounts that break these rules.

shut down at any time, and accounts and chips may be lost when that happens.
