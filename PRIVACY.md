# Privacy

What poker collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

## What we collect

- **Your email and password.** Sign-in is handled by Google Identity Platform
  (Firebase Authentication). The game server never sees your password.
- **Your username**, and when you joined and were last online.
- **Your games:** the hands you played (your cards, the actions and results),
  your chip balance and its history, invites, and messages you send in table chat.
- **Connection details:** your IP address and request times appear in server and
  sign-in logs, which are used to stop abuse.

## Who can see it

- Everyone can see your username, your seat and chips at tables, table chat,
  and your place on the leaderboard.
- Other players see your cards only when you show them at showdown, including
  in their hand history.
- Admins can see your email, your account status and your games, to run the
  game and stop abuse.

## Where it's kept

On Google Cloud in the United States (Oregon). Logs are kept for 30 days. Game
records are kept while your account exists.

Nothing is sold or shared for advertising, and there are no third-party trackers.

## Deleting your account

Open an issue in this repository with your username (not your email), and we'll
confirm by email before deleting your account and its data.
